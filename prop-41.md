# Proposition 41 — Audits of Programs Funded by New State Special Taxes

**Data date (FPPC "Last Modified"): October 6, 2026**

**Official title:** "Requires Audits of Programs Funded by New State Special Taxes." (Linked to the
Props 40/42 tax fight — see `prop-40.md`.)

**Totals (SOS, through 10/06/2026):** Support **$58,376,446** · Oppose **$703,494**

---

## Support

### Committee: Yes on 41 – Californians for Transparency and Accountability (#1488423)
SOS committee total: **$58,376,446**. FPPC contributors (filed fact). Lines sum to **$58,371,808 ≈ 100.0%**.

| Contributor | Amount | Share of committee |
|---|---|---|
| Building a Better California *(traced below)* | $58,350,000 | 99.96% |
| Reform California with Carl De Maio | $21,807.92 | 0.04% |

**This committee is 99.96% one donor: Building a Better California.** The committee name
("Transparency and Accountability, a coalition of small businesses, taxpayers...") does not reflect this.

**Traced donor — Building a Better California ($58,350,000):** the large tech-funded committee whose
reported donors include **Sergey Brin** (≈$102M cumulative, its largest), **John Doerr** (≈$20M) and
**Vinod Khosla** (≈$13M) (*SF Chronicle / Yahoo News, Aug–Sep 2026*). Amounts are committee-wide totals,
not a Prop 41 earmark. Same source funds Props 42 (support, $97.8M), 40 (oppose, $88.5M), 37 and 45.

## Oppose

**SOS-reported opposition: $703,494**, entirely the committee
"Tax the Ultra-Rich Now, Yes on 3 & 40, No on 41 & 42." No FPPC top-10 detail beyond its shared-list
presence.

> **Double-counting trap — important:** the FPPC page also lists the **"Yes on 40 – Billionaire Tax Now"
> committee (#1484470)** and its ~$31.5M of SEIU-UHW money under Prop 41's *opposing* side, because that
> committee's formal position is "No on 41 & 42." **But the SOS does not count those dollars as Prop 41
> opposition** — the SOS Prop 41 oppose total is only **$703,494**. Those SEIU-UHW dollars are the same
> money reported as **Prop 40 support** (all asterisked). **Do not add the ~$31.5M SEIU figure to Prop 41's
> opposition total** — that would double-count the Prop 40 money. See `prop-40.md`.

## Notes
- **Asterisk / double-counting:** the SEIU-UHW "Yes on 40" committee is cross-listed here but its money
  belongs to Prop 40; "Tax the Ultra-Rich Now" ($703,494) spans Props 3/40/41/42. Do not sum across measures.
- **Interests to be aware of (inference):** support is funded by the same tech-donor committee that
  opposes the Prop 40 wealth tax; the formal opposition is the union-side committee backing Prop 40.
