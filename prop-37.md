# Proposition 37 — Loan Program for Middle-Income Home Buyers

**Data date (FPPC "Last Modified"): October 6, 2026**

**Official title:** "Creates Loan Program for Middle-Income Buyers of Qualified New Homes.
Initiative Statute." (Proponents' name: "California Middle-Class Homeownership and Family Home
Construction Act.")

**Totals (SOS, through 10/06/2026):** Support **$19,618,927** · Oppose **$0**

---

## Support

| California Forever Operations, Inc. | $50,000 | 0.3% |

**Headline:** two intermediaries dominate — **Homeownership for Families** (Realtor money) and
**Building a Better California** (tech money).

> **Discrepancy flag (verify on Cal-Access):** the FPPC top-10 lines sum to **≈ $22,616,989**, which
> exceeds this committee's SOS total ($19,618,927); the two largest lines alone ($14.85M + $6.0M =
> $20.85M) already exceed it. The FPPC figures are evidently cumulative / aggregated differently from
> the SOS receipts. Percentages are each line ÷ the SOS total and do **not** add to 100%. Do not sum.

### Traced donor — Homeownership for Families (#1400190), contributed $14,850,000
This contributor is itself a committee. Its own FPPC top donors (filed fact):

| Donor into Homeownership for Families | Amount |
|---|---|
| California Association of Realtors Issues Mobilization PAC | $5,000,000 |
| National Association of Realtors | $2,000,000 |

So the money reaching Yes on 37 through this channel originates with the **California Association of
Realtors** and the **National Association of Realtors**. (Note: the $5M + $2M filed into Homeownership
for Families is less than the $14.85M it passed to Yes on 37 — a cumulative/timing mismatch; verify on
Cal-Access.)

### Traced donor — Building a Better California, contributed $6,000,000
FPPC note names **Sergey Brin** and **L. John Doerr III** as donors to Building a Better California
(no split given). This is the same large tech-funded committee active on Props 1, 40, 41, 42, and 45.
News-sourced context: Building a Better California has raised well over $200M, with Sergey Brin its
largest donor (≈$102M) and John Doerr ≈$20M (*SF Chronicle / Fortune, 2026*). These are totals for
the committee, **not** the amount earmarked to Prop 37.

*California Forever Operations, Inc.* (general knowledge, labeled) is the company behind a proposed
new city in Solano County.

### Verification (cross-check, 2026-10-08; Cal-Access itself blocks automated access)
CalMatters (2026) reports Prop 37 "was placed on the ballot by a coalition of **real estate interests
and labor unions**" — corroborating the funding structure (Realtors via Homeownership for Families +
carpenter PACs + Building a Better California). The exact per-donor dollar amounts ($14.85M via
Homeownership for Families, $6M from Building a Better California) were **not independently confirmed
in news** and, as flagged above, the FPPC top-10 lines exceed the SOS committee total; confirm the
itemized receipts on Cal-Access.
