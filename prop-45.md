## Notes
- **Support vs. oppose discrepancy with FPPC:** the FPPC lists one extra support committee (BRIDGE PAC,
  PG&E $2.4M) and one extra oppose committee ("Protect California Communities," #1479255) that are **not**
  separate lines in the SOS totals. Reported here as FPPC facts and flagged; the SOS two-committee totals
  on each side sum exactly to the SOS support/oppose totals. Verify on Cal-Access.
- **Intra-side double-counting (opposition):** $1.5M transfer from #1492264 to #1493079 counted in both.
- **Interests to be aware of (inference):** support is funded by homebuilders, a tech-donor committee,
  utilities and realtors (parties whose projects would face lighter environmental review); opposition is
  funded by environmental groups and building-trades labor plus a major individual philanthropist.
