> **Discrepancy flag (verify on Cal-Access):** the FPPC top-10 lines above sum to **≈ $48,225,000**,
> which is *larger* than this committee's SOS-reported total ($44,174,753) and larger than the entire
> measure's SOS support total ($46,260,006). The FPPC figures appear to be cumulative/aggregated
> differently from the SOS receipts. The "share of committee" percentages are each line ÷ the SOS
> total and therefore do **not** add cleanly to 100%. Treat the amounts as "as filed with FPPC" and
> confirm the detail manually. Do not sum.

  is the same committee money counted on Props 3 (support), 40 (support), 41 (oppose), 42 (oppose).

  Props 3 (support), 39 (oppose), 43 (oppose). **Do not add these across measures.**

  education and public-employee labor organizations whose members are funded by the schools/health
  programs the measure would support.
