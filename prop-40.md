# Proposition 40 — One-Time Tax on Certain Individuals and Trusts ("Billionaire Tax")

**Data date (FPPC "Last Modified"): October 6, 2026**

**Official title:** "Imposes One-Time Tax on Certain Taxpayers. Initiative Constitutional Amendment
and Statute." (Proponents' name: "Billionaire Tax.")

**Totals (SOS, through 10/06/2026):** Support **$32,213,823** · Oppose **$99,596,279**

Props 40, 41 and 42 form one linked fight: **the Prop 40 support committee simultaneously opposes
41 and 42, and the Prop 41/42 support committees are the same Building-a-Better-California money that
opposes 40.** The same dollars therefore appear across all three measures — see the double-counting
flags here and in `prop-41.md` and `prop-42.md`.

## Notes
- **Asterisk / double-counting (critical on this measure):** every line in the Yes on 40 committee is
  asterisked because that committee **also opposes Props 41 and 42**. The same SEIU-UHW dollars appear
  as *support* on Prop 40 and as *opposition* on Props 41 and 42. "Tax the Ultra-Rich Now" ($703,494)
  likewise spans Props 3/40/41/42. **Do not add any of these figures across measures.**
- Building a Better California's ~$88.5M opposing Prop 40 is the same committee that *supports* Props 41,
  42, 37 and 45 — different checks from the same source. See cross-measure summary.
- **Interests to be aware of (inference from sources):** support is funded by a health-care workers'
  union; the opposition's dominant funder is a committee of wealthy individuals of the kind the one-time
  tax would reach (per news reporting on the measure's $1B+ wealth threshold).
