# Proposition 39 — Voter ID and Citizenship Verification

**Data date (FPPC "Last Modified"): October 6, 2026**

**Official title:** "Prohibits Citizens from Voting Unless They Present Government-Issued
Identification. Initiative Constitutional Amendment." (SOS short form also: "Establishes Additional
Voter Identification and Citizenship Verification Requirements.")

**Totals (SOS, through 10/06/2026):** Support **$15,785,413** · Oppose **$29,637,418**

This is the most-contested measure by committee count: one support committee against five opposition
committees. Opposition out-raised support by nearly 2 to 1.

## Verification (Cal-Access cross-check, performed 2026-10-08)
- **Direct Cal-Access: not accessible.** The committee detail page (#1479607) returns a bot-detection
  page and blocks automated access; it was not bypassed. SOS Power Search (powersearch.sos.ca.gov) is
  reachable but did not expose committee-level ledgers via a plain request.
- **Cross-checked against CalMatters (Oct 2026), which reports from the Cal-Access filings:** the
  support-total discrepancy flagged above is **confirmed and explained** — the $17M Uihlein figure and
  the "more than $36M" raised are **campaign-wide** (spanning the "Reform California" signature-drive
  committee *and* "Californians for Voter ID"); CalMatters explicitly does not separate the two. That is
  why $17M exceeds the single SOS committee total of $15,785,413. CalMatters also confirms **Joe Gebbia
  $8M**, a major donor that sits outside this committee's FPPC top-10.
- CalMatters itemizes Uihlein gifts as $6.5M (08/04/2026) + $6.5M (09/10/2026) + $1.0M (12/16/2025) =
  $14.0M documented, against a stated $17M total (remainder not itemized in the source).
- **Opposition figures reconcile** with CalMatters: California Democratic Party ~$5.5M = $3,204,703
  (Block Election Rigging) + $2,250,000 (United Against Prop 39); Graton Rancheria $2M; Patty Quillin
  $1.5M; Quinn Delaney $1.5M; SEIU ~$1M. ✅
- *Still requires direct Cal-Access confirmation:* the exact split of Uihlein's and Reform California's
  dollars between the two support committees, and the committee of record for Gebbia's $8M.

## Notes
- **Asterisk / double-counting:** "Communities for an Affordable California" ($1,090,000) is the same
  committee money counted on Props 3 (support), 39 (oppose), 43 (oppose). Do not add across measures.
- **Intra-side double-counting (opposition):** the $2,000,000 transfer from "Block Election Rigging"
  into "United Against Prop. 39" is counted in both committees within the $29.64M SOS oppose total.
- **Support-total discrepancy:** see the boxed flag above — Uihlein's $17M exceeds the SOS committee total.
- **Interests to be aware of (inference from sources):** support is led by out-of-state and tech donors;
  opposition is led by the California Democratic Party, labor unions, a tribe, and civil-liberties groups.
