# Proposition 42 — Prohibits New State Personal Property Taxes / Certain Retroactive Taxes

**Data date (FPPC "Last Modified"): October 6, 2026**

**Official title:** "Prohibits New State Personal Property Taxes and Certain Retroactive State Taxes.
Initiative Constitutional Amendment." (Linked to the Props 40/41 tax fight — see `prop-40.md`.)

**Totals (SOS, through 10/06/2026):** Support **$110,827,037** · Oppose **$703,494**

This is the **single most-funded support side of any 2026 measure** by SOS total.

---

## Support

### Committee: Yes on 42 – Protect Retirement & Life Savings (#1488418)
SOS committee total: **$110,827,037**. FPPC contributors (filed fact):

| Contributor | Amount | Share of committee ($110.83M) |
|---|---|---|
| Building a Better California *(traced below)* | $97,800,000 | 88.2% |
| Reform California with Carl De Maio | $22,537.26 | 0.02% |

> **Note on the gap:** the FPPC list shows only these two contributors of $10,000+ for this committee,
> totaling ≈$97.82M, while the SOS committee total is $110.83M — about **$13M is not itemized** on the
> FPPC top-10 list (either below the $10k line or a later/other receipt). Verify on Cal-Access.

**This committee is at least ~88% one donor: Building a Better California.** The committee name
("Protect Retirement & Life Savings, a coalition of retirees, blue-collar workers, seniors,
veterans...") does not reflect this.

**Traced donor — Building a Better California ($97,800,000):** the large tech-funded committee whose
reported donors include **Sergey Brin** (≈$102M cumulative, its largest), **John Doerr** (≈$20M) and
**Vinod Khosla** (≈$13M) (*SF Chronicle / Yahoo News, Aug–Sep 2026*). Amounts are committee-wide totals,
not a Prop 42 earmark. Same source funds Props 41 (support, $58.35M), 40 (oppose, $88.5M), 37 and 45.

## Oppose

**SOS-reported opposition: $703,494**, entirely the committee
"Tax the Ultra-Rich Now, Yes on 3 & 40, No on 41 & 42." No FPPC top-10 detail beyond its shared-list
presence.

> **Double-counting trap — important:** the FPPC page also lists the **"Yes on 40 – Billionaire Tax Now"
> committee (#1484470)** and its ~$31.5M of SEIU-UHW money under Prop 42's *opposing* side (its formal
> position is "No on 41 & 42"). **The SOS does not count those dollars as Prop 42 opposition** — the SOS
> Prop 42 oppose total is only **$703,494**. Those SEIU-UHW dollars are the same money reported as **Prop 40
> support** (all asterisked). **Do not add the ~$31.5M SEIU figure to Prop 42's opposition total.** See
> `prop-40.md`.

## Notes
- **Asterisk / double-counting:** as with Prop 41, the SEIU-UHW "Yes on 40" committee is cross-listed on
  the oppose side but its money belongs to Prop 40; "Tax the Ultra-Rich Now" ($703,494) spans Props
  3/40/41/42. Do not sum across measures.
- **Interests to be aware of (inference):** support is the same tech-donor committee opposing the Prop 40
  wealth tax; formal opposition is the union-side committee backing Prop 40.
