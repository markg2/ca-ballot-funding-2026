# Proposition 38 — Bonds for Immunology Medical Research

**Data date (FPPC "Last Modified"): October 6, 2026**

**Official title:** "Authorizes Bonds for Immunology Medical Research. Initiative Statute."

**Totals (SOS, through 10/06/2026):** Support **$64,215,472** · Oppose **$3,782**

---

## Support

The SOS lists two support committees; both trace almost entirely to one person.

### Committee: Yes on 38 – Californians for Life-Saving Immunology Research and Cures (#1485847)
SOS committee total: **$51,215,472**. FPPC top contributors (filed fact):

| Contributor | Amount | Share of committee ($51.22M) |
|---|---|---|
| Gary K. Michelson | $25,000,000 | 48.8% |
| Michelson Center for Public Policy *(donor: Gary K. Michelson)* | $21,200,000 | 41.4% |
| Meyer Luskin | $5,000,000 | 9.8% |

Top-3 lines sum to **$51,200,000 ≈ 100.0%** of the committee total — this committee reconciles cleanly.

**Traced donor — Michelson Center for Public Policy ($21,200,000):** FPPC note names **Gary K.
Michelson** as its donor. Combined with his $25,000,000 direct gift, Gary Michelson is behind
roughly **$46.2M of $51.2M (90%)** of this committee.

### Committee: Michelson Center for Public Policy, Inc. – Yes on 38 (501(c)(4))
SOS committee total: **$13,000,000**. FPPC top contributor (filed fact):

| Contributor | Amount | Share of committee ($13.0M) |
|---|---|---|
| Gary K. Michelson | $10,000,000 | 76.9% |

**Headline:** Proposition 38 is funded overwhelmingly by **Gary K. Michelson** (directly and through
his Michelson Center for Public Policy), with a $5,000,000 gift from **Meyer Luskin** the only other
large donor. (General knowledge, labeled: Gary Michelson is a retired surgeon, inventor and
philanthropist; Meyer Luskin is a businessman and philanthropist.)

## Oppose
