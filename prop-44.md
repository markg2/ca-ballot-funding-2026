# Proposition 44 — Community Health Clinics: 90% Spending on Program Services

**Data date (FPPC "Last Modified"): October 6, 2026**

**Official title:** "Requires Community Health Clinics to Spend 90% of Revenue on Program Services.
Initiative Statute."

**Totals (SOS, through 10/06/2026):** Support **$10,781,565** · Oppose **$21,609,280**

Opposition out-raised support roughly 2 to 1.

---

## Support

### Committee: Yes on Prop 44, sponsored by SEIU-UHW (#1483073)
SOS committee total: **$10,781,565**. FPPC contributor (filed fact — asterisked, SEIU-UHW is active
across measures):

| Contributor | Amount (as filed, FPPC) |
|---|---|
| SEIU-UHW West Political Issues Committee | $16,929,633.88 * |

> **Discrepancy flag (verify on Cal-Access):** the FPPC line for the SEIU-UHW Political Issues Committee
> ($16,929,633.88) is *larger than this committee's SOS total ($10,781,565)*. The FPPC figure appears to
> be the contributor's cumulative giving rather than a single receipt into the Prop 44 committee. A
> per-committee percentage is therefore not computed. What is clear: **Prop 44 support is funded entirely
> by SEIU – United Healthcare Workers West.** (This is a *different* SEIU-UHW contribution from the one on
> Prop 40; the asterisk flags that the donor is active on multiple measures, but these are not the same
> dollars.)

### Verification (cross-check, 2026-10-08; Cal-Access itself blocks automated access)
CalMatters and KPBS (2026) report that SEIU-UHW "has raised **about $17 million** to support the
campaign, mainly from union coffers" — **corroborating the FPPC figure ($16,929,633.88)**, not the SOS
committee total ($10,781,565).

> **Resolved direction of the discrepancy:** news (~$17M) matches the **FPPC** side; **the SOS
> contribution-totals page appears to understate** the Prop 44 support campaign. Treat ~$17M as the
> better estimate of SEIU-UHW's total campaign money; the $10.78M SOS figure may reflect only receipts
> into the candidate committee of record. Confirm on Cal-Access.

News also confirms the **opposition composition** — California Primary Care Association, California
Medical Association and community clinic groups — consistent with the clinic donors below. (The
individual clinic amounts, e.g. AltaMed $5.47M, were not separately confirmed in news.)

## Notes
- **Asterisk / double-counting:** the SEIU-UHW Political Issues Committee is asterisked because SEIU-UHW
  funds committees across several measures (notably Prop 40). Its Prop 44 contribution and its Prop 40
  contribution are *separate* contributions; do not sum the SEIU-UHW lines across measures as if they
  were one gift, and note the FPPC figure here exceeds the SOS committee total (see flag).
- **Interests to be aware of (inference from sources):** support is a health-care workers' union;
  opposition is the regulated clinics (and their trade association) that would face the spending mandate.
